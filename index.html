About me

Engineer by training, analyst by instinct

From electronics labs in Bangalore to enterprise data platforms in the U.S., I've always liked turning signals into something people can act on.

I'm a Data Engineer and BI Analyst based in the New York City area, with a Master's in Business Analytics (Global Supply Chain Analytics concentration) from the University of New Haven and a Bachelor's in Electronics & Communication Engineering from RNS Institute of Technology.

My engineering roots taught me to measure carefully: at Tata Steel I streamed sensor data to the cloud, and at VI Solutions I automated hardware testing in LabVIEW. My final-year project, a breast cancer classifier, reached 98% accuracy and pulled me fully into data.

At Populus Health Technologies I grew from IT intern to the person who owned the analytics suite: Looker dashboards and weekly market reports for 30+ clients and internal teams with HIPAA-compliant handling, LookML semantic models, and a resilient Python pipeline for 500K+ NPI Registry records. I also ran the technical side of digital health campaign launches, from creative setup and 100+ test pages to measurement-vendor integrations.

Today at NStarX I design the analytics layer for Agentra, an expense management product: a 12-table star schema, 25+ DAX measures, and a compliance engine that flagged $94K in spend.

Machine learning runs through my work: Prophet, XGBoost and Azure AutoML forecasts for the Port Authority, ML-versus-ARIMA benchmarking at NStarX, and an SVM classifier at 98% accuracy. I completed The AI Engineer Course 2026 and use generative AI and LLM tools, such as Claude Code, in my day-to-day development.

I enjoy the whole path from raw source to final dashboard, and I work best in collaborative, agile teams that care about data people can trust.
